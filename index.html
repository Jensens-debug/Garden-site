Our Story

Started with one raised bed and a lot of stubborn tomatoes

The Real Food Gardens began with a single overgrown backyard plot and a promise: if we were going to grow food, it was going to actually get eaten. What started as one family's kitchen garden turned into a design-build studio, then a coaching program, then a small team of people who genuinely like showing up in your yard with dirt under their nails.

"We believe a garden should feed you — not become one more thing on your to-do list. That's why we build the plan, the beds, and the habit, all at once."
