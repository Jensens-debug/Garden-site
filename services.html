Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need full sun to grow vegetables?

Most fruiting crops (tomatoes, peppers, squash) want 6+ hours of direct sun. If your yard gets less, we'll design around leafy greens, herbs, and root vegetables instead — we'll tell you honestly what will and won't thrive.

How long does installation take?

A standard 3-4 bed backyard garden usually takes one to two days once materials are on-site. Larger installs with trellises, pathways, or irrigation can take a week.

Do you work with schools and businesses, not just homes?

Yes — a good share of our installs are school gardens, office courtyards, and community spaces. Pricing and packages are the same; timelines just depend on the site.

What if I already have raised beds?

Totally fine — coaching and maintenance plans work with whatever's already in the ground. We'll assess what you have during the first visit.

What areas do you currently serve?

We currently install in 12 metro areas and offer remote design consults everywhere else. Check availability for your zip code on the contact page.
