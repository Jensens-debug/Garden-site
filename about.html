Our Story

Started with one raised bed and a lot of stubborn tomatoes

We're a small design-build-coach studio that believes a kitchen garden should feed you, not just decorate the backyard.

Founded 2017

A backyard project that refused to stay small

It began with one overgrown plot and a promise: if we were going to grow food, it was going to actually get eaten. Neighbors started asking for help with their own beds, then schools, then small businesses looking to turn unused courtyards into something productive.

Today we're a team of designers, builders, and garden coaches working across a dozen cities — but the goal hasn't changed. We want your garden to feed you, teach you, and still be standing (and thriving) five years from now.

"A garden should feed you — not become one more thing on your to-do list."
Milestones

How we got here

2017

Founded in a backyard

One raised bed, a handful of tomato plants, and a lot of trial and error that turned into a service other people started asking to pay for.

2019

First school garden program

Partnered with a local elementary school to design and install a garden now woven into their science curriculum.

2021

Launched the raised bed & trellis line

Started building our own cedar beds and signature trellises in-house after getting tired of recommending products that didn't hold up.

2023

Opened in a second city

Grew the crew and coaching team to serve a second metro area, with more on the way.

2026

500+ gardens and counting

Now serving 12 cities with a full design, build, and coaching team — still visiting every garden we install.
